# Mission 6 — "Black Notebook"

> *"I just wanted to get out. Funny. You did too."*
> — Sebastian Volkov

---

## Logline

Volkov ran for Regatul Kim Kim and got as far as the border before it slammed
shut. Now he's trapped in the no-man's-land rail yard between two countries,
with the Presidential Guard closing in and the last copy of the notebook Biribel
stole from him in Mission 1. Biribel gets there first — just. The notebook
proves two things: **Retardovici ordered Route 12, and Vasile's codes carried it
out.** Then Ucilev's shot comes out of the dark, and a train leaves without its
passenger. Biribel walks back with the truth and a choice.

---

## Where We Pick Up (Recap from M5 Ending)

1. **Retardovici rules by decree.** Balaceanu is dead; parliament is gone.
2. **Maru is free** — because Biribel disobeyed the Guard.
3. **Ucilev let Biribel live.** "They haven't told me to yet."
4. **Vasile covered for him.** Biribel still trusts him.

**M6 cold open: Day ~10.** Maru's people intercept Guard traffic: a "high-value
deserter" pinned at the Kim Kim border crossing. The description fits Volkov.

---

## Setting

**The Kim Kim border crossing, a freezing night.**

- **The customs post** — abandoned Acaristan-side building: files, stamps, a
  frozen queue of cars nobody came back for.
- **No-man's-land** — a 300 m strip of floodlights and razor wire between two
  closed gates.
- **The rail yard** — freight cars, a signal tower, and one passenger train
  Kim Kim is still letting out. Volkov is hiding in a boxcar.

---

## The Spine — The Truth, and Who Pays For It

M6 is the hinge of the campaign. It hands the player **both** reasons for the
final choice in one object — the notebook — and makes it hurt by killing the
man who tried to tell them all along.

### The Volkov truth
Everyone — the player included — thought Volkov was a black-market rat. He
wasn't. He was an IPA logistics clerk who saw the Route 12 movement orders
**before** the ambush, understood what they were, and tried to sell the proof to
anyone who could stop it, to buy his family's way out of Acaristan. The warning
was in the notebook.

### The irony
In **Mission 1**, Biribel intercepted the handoff and gave the notebook to
**Vasile** — the one man it named. Vasile rushed Biribel to Route 12 the same
night, "no debrief." **Biribel's first mission doomed his own squad.** Volkov
always knew. He never told him.

## Characters & Arcs

### Biribel
Learns that the man he trusts most sold his squad, that the hero President gave
the order, and that his own hands carried the notebook to the traitor. Ends the
mission with nothing left but the choice.

### Sebastian Volkov
His last mission, and his only chance to be seen as what he was. Frightened,
dry, tired, decent. He doesn't ask Biribel for forgiveness and doesn't make
speeches. He hands over the notebook, admits the truth, and steps toward the
train. He doesn't make it. **His death is quiet, not dramatic** — Biribel
catches him, and the train leaves.

### Ucilev Nadgob Ierdna
Takes the shot from the signal tower: one round, professional, no gloating.
Afterward, Biribel finds his file in the customs post's Guard terminal and
learns the name behind the mirror: **Bogdan Velicu** — same KRAP intake
as Biribel, top of the class, listed *killed in action* at Y-2. The state's
version of Maru's faked death.

### Captain Vasile
Radio only. When Biribel asks him straight, Vasile doesn't lie well enough:
*"I chose the many. You'd have done the same."* Then he makes the offer that
sets up Missions 7 and 8.

### Maru (MAG)
Flies Biribel in and out (sets up the helicopter in M8). Reads the notebook in
silence and says only: *"Now you know what we've been fighting."*

## Mission Phases

| # | Phase | Objective | Beat |
|---|-------|-----------|------|
| 1 | **Insertion** (cutscene) | — | Maru's helicopter drops Biribel in the hills above the crossing under a static-line canopy. 3D. |
| 2 | **The Customs Post** | Get through the abandoned post. | Top-down; Guard sweeps it. Find the frozen queue of cars — families who didn't make it. |
| 3 | **No-Man's-Land** | Cross the floodlit strip. | Stealth: floodlight sweeps, a timed gap. Spotted = alarm, waves from both gates. |
| 4 | **The Rail Yard** | Find Volkov before the Guard does. | Boxcar search while Guard teams close in. |
| 5 | **The Boxcar** (3D dialogue) | — | Volkov and Biribel talk as equals for the first time. The notebook. |
| 6 | **The Train** (3D, interactive) | Get Volkov to the train. | Short in-engine escort: suppress the Guard on the platform. Clear the lane and he reaches the train — where Ucilev's shot kills him. Leave the squad up and they cut him down in the open; the run fails and retries. |
| 7 | **The File** (ending film) | — | Ucilev's real identity, shown in the terminal insert. |
| 8 | **The Call** (ending film) | — | Vasile confesses; makes the offer. Biribel doesn't answer. Maru sends him to ground (→ M7, Bodi's farm). |

**Fail state:** death reloads the latest phase checkpoint. Biribel is not
immortal in the train: Guard return fire has no 1-HP floor, and going down
freezes the fight and plays a continuous first-person 3D collapse in the train
scene (the eye drops, rolls and tips up; no cut to a body cam) before the
terminal screen, which respawns at the train checkpoint. Volkov dies in every
run: the player's job is not to save him but to get him to the train. Clear
the lane and Ucilev's single shot kills him at the train (the canon catch and
ending). Leave two or more Guards up and they cut him down in the open; the
mission fails and retries the train checkpoint instead of progressing. The
last playable beat is the train; the file and the call are told by the ending
film, so the finale never repeats as a second top-down errand.

## Key Scene — The Boxcar

```
VOLKOV   "You took this from me once. Read it this time."
BIRIBEL  "...Route 12. Movement order. Authorization -" (beat) "That's Vasile's code."
VOLKOV   "I tried to sell it to anyone who'd stop it. You were the one who showed up."
BIRIBEL  "You knew. In the warehouse. You knew where it would go."
VOLKOV   "I knew who you'd give it to. I didn't know how to tell you without you shooting me."
VOLKOV   "I just wanted to get out. Funny. You did too."
```

## Key Scene — The Train (3D)

```
(Platform. Snow. Volkov runs for the open carriage door. Biribel covers.)
VOLKOV   "Kim Kim has terrible coffee, I hear. I'm going to drink all of it."
```

## What This Resolves

- Volkov's story: from "rat" to the man who tried to stop Route 12.
- Who ordered Route 12 (Retardovici) and who carried it out (Vasile).

## Technical Notes

- **Package:** `src/missions/m6/` (`meta`, `maps`, `decor`, `stealth`, `world`,
  `flow`, `systems`, `draw`, plus lazy `train3d` and `m6_audio`), same facade
  pattern; stage driver `STATE.mission6_stage`. Mission-owned procedural audio
  is synthesized at runtime (`M6FilmAudio` / `M6TrainAudio`); there are no
  recorded M6 voice takes, so dialogue is captioned and radio text.
- **Maps:** three MapBuilder-authored `list[str]` maps, all passing
  `validate()`: customs post 30×20, no-man's-land 40×18 and rail yard 46×26.
  `bind_stage()` rebinds the live `MISSION6_MAP` /
  `M6_STRUCTURE_MAP`, so collision, minimap and draw never read a stale grid.
- **Flow:** five playable stages — customs → noman → rail → boxcar → train —
  then the ending film. Boxcar is a non-saving mid-cutscene inside the rail
  checkpoint; the train hands straight to `STATE.request_mission6_ending`.
  The terminal file and Vasile's call are ending-film beats, not top-down
  stages. Death restarts at the deepest checkpoint reached (train → rail →
  noman → customs).
- **3D films:** thirteen `engine3d` scenes, registered in
  `core.cutscene_registry` and rendered by `cutscenes/m6films.py` on the
  `cutscenes/m6world3d.py` sets: a four-shot ridge insertion (`m6_insert_*`),
  the five-shot boxcar conversation (`m6_boxcar_*`), and the exact ending chain
  (`m6_customs_terminal`, four `m6_radio_vasile` shots, `m6_radio_maru`,
  `m6_helicopter`, then the title card). Every scene has a hand-authored 2D
  fallback; a 3D failure permanently degrades the run to 2D, and
  `ACARISTAN_CUTSCENE3D=0` forces it. Exterior sets bake once per shot rig;
  the boxcar and office interiors pack as one merged mesh per frame, keeping
  the 3D cuts around 20–35 ms at the 600×273 internal buffer.
- **Staging and motion:** insertion is a hover-drop over the ridge. The Huey
  sways and bobs in the wide (`hover_pos`), with Biribel riding the open door
  and his soles on the skid. He drops twelve metres into the border valley
  under a static-line canopy, collapses it on landing and walks downhill while
  the Huey climbs away. Volkov rises off the crate on planted feet and
  walks out through the boxcar door, and offers the notebook open with both
  hands before Biribel takes it. `m6world3d` owns the motion kit
  (`sit_pose`, `planted_pos`, `walk`, `walk_slip`) and a `SOLIDS` registry of
  set AABBs, so the renderer, the probe and the tests share one geometry.
  Per-shot light rigs keep bright practicals off the face mark. The ending
  camera sits on the ridge over the capital: skyline, sodium haze, blinking
  aviation lights, dark hills, and a banked Huey turning toward them under a
  translucent two-blade rotor blur.
- **Train:** `missions/m6/train3d.py` is a live interactive scene, not a film:
  shoulder camera with clamped mouse yaw/pitch, K9 fire/reload against shared
  ammo, hitscan Guards whose return fire is lethal (no 1-HP floor; death
  freezes the fight and plays a continuous first-person 3D collapse in the
  same scene - eye drop, roll, sky tilt - before the terminal screen, whose
  restart respawns at the train checkpoint; the frozen 3D frame stays visible
  behind that screen). The
  platform roster is five archetypes (line rifle, breacher,
  marksman, heavy, officer) with distinct rigs, weapons, burst patterns, IFF
  strobes and cover-to-cover advances; two or more living Guards pin Volkov
  and cost him up to 0.7 s of his run. At 16.55 the branch is decided by the
  platform: with two or more Guards still up, they cut Volkov down in the
  open and the run ends in a failure terminal/retry (never the ending film).
  With the lane clear (at most one survivor), Volkov reaches the train and
  Ucilev's unavoidable signal-tower shot kills him; the catch scene and
  ending follow. E/SPACE/RETURN skips after two seconds (H any time);
  completion, skip, or exception all hand off to the ending film exactly
  once — failure paths never do.
- **Floodlights:** ground-projected, wall-occluded cone sweeps (16 px tile
  raycast against the bound structure map), four towers in no-man's-land and
  two in the rail yard. Accumulated exposure latches the alarm once at
  `EXPOSURE_ALARM_S = 0.4`; the alarm alerts present Guards and spawns waves
  from both gates, capped at `MAX_ALARM_ENEMIES = 6`. Detection starts a fight,
  never a death or teleport.
- **State:** `m6_exposure`, `m6_alarm`, `m6_alarm_triggered`,
  `m6_wave_count`, `m6_volkov_dead`, `m6_train_active/time/result`,
  `m6_train_overrun/guards_left`, `m6_train_death`, `request_m6_cutscene`,
  the four checkpoint flags and
  `request_mission6_ending`. The Duty/Revenge route choice is still chosen at
  the end of M7 (`STATE.m8_route`).
- **Verification:** `tools/smoke_test.py` covers the map contract, stage
  ladder, stealth alarm, films and TrainScene; `tests/unit/test_m6_campaign.py`
  covers geometry, exposure, the shot contract, train mechanics, checkpoints,
  and the staging pass: every 100 ms of the boxcar, radio and door-seat shots
  clears the registered set AABBs, seated pelvis/feet meet the seat and skid,
  gait stance feet slip under 2 cm, rotor blur reaches the blade radius, skids
  stay above `height_at`, and three software frames hold head-region luma in
  the readable band. `tools/m6_shots.py --probe` prints face luma, solid
  penetration and walk slip per frame for visual review.
