# Mission 6 — "Black Notebook"

> *"I just wanted to get out. Funny. You did too."*
> — Sebastian Volkov

---

## Logline

Volkov ran for Regatul Kim Kim and got as far as the border before it slammed
shut. Now he's trapped in the no-man's-land rail yard between two countries,
with the Presidential Guard closing in and the last copy of the notebook Biribel
stole from him in Mission 1. Biribel gets there first — just. The notebook
proves two things: **Retardovici ordered Route 12, and Vasile's codes carried it
out.** Then Ucilev's shot comes out of the dark, and a train leaves without its
passenger. Biribel walks back with the truth and a choice.

---

## Where We Pick Up (Recap from M5 Ending)

1. **Retardovici rules by decree.** Balaceanu is dead; parliament is gone.
2. **Maru is free** — because Biribel disobeyed the Guard.
3. **Ucilev let Biribel live.** "They haven't told me to yet."
4. **Vasile covered for him.** Biribel still trusts him.

**M6 cold open: Day ~10.** Maru's people intercept Guard traffic: a "high-value
deserter" pinned at the Kim Kim border crossing. The description fits Volkov.

---

## Setting

**The Kim Kim border crossing, a freezing night.**

- **The customs post** — abandoned Acaristan-side building: files, stamps, a
  frozen queue of cars nobody came back for.
- **No-man's-land** — a 300 m strip of floodlights and razor wire between two
  closed gates.
- **The rail yard** — freight cars, a signal tower, and one passenger train
  Kim Kim is still letting out. Volkov is hiding in a boxcar.

---

## The Spine — The Truth, and Who Pays For It

M6 is the hinge of the campaign. It hands the player **both** reasons for the
final choice in one object — the notebook — and makes it hurt by killing the
man who tried to tell them all along.

### The Volkov truth
Everyone — the player included — thought Volkov was a black-market rat. He
wasn't. He was an IPA logistics clerk who saw the Route 12 movement orders
**before** the ambush, understood what they were, and tried to sell the proof to
anyone who could stop it, to buy his family's way out of Acaristan. The warning
was in the notebook.

### The irony
In **Mission 1**, Biribel intercepted the handoff and gave the notebook to
**Vasile** — the one man it named. Vasile rushed Biribel to Route 12 the same
night, "no debrief." **Biribel's first mission doomed his own squad.** Volkov
always knew. He never told him.

## Characters & Arcs

### Biribel
Learns that the man he trusts most sold his squad, that the hero President gave
the order, and that his own hands carried the notebook to the traitor. Ends the
mission with nothing left but the choice.

### Sebastian Volkov
His last mission, and his only chance to be seen as what he was. Frightened,
dry, tired, decent. He doesn't ask Biribel for forgiveness and doesn't make
speeches. He hands over the notebook, admits the truth, and steps toward the
train. He doesn't make it. **His death is quiet, not dramatic** — Biribel
catches him, and the train leaves.

### Ucilev Nadgob Ierdna
Takes the shot from the signal tower: one round, professional, no gloating.
Afterward, Biribel finds his file in the customs post's Guard terminal and
learns the name behind the mirror: **Andrei Bogdan Velicu** — same KRAP intake
as Biribel, top of the class, listed *killed in action* at Y-2. The state's
version of Maru's faked death.

### Captain Vasile
Radio only. When Biribel asks him straight, Vasile doesn't lie well enough:
*"I chose the many. You'd have done the same."* Then he makes the offer that
sets up Missions 7 and 8.

### Maru (MAG)
Flies Biribel in and out (sets up the helicopter in M8). Reads the notebook in
silence and says only: *"Now you know what we've been fighting."*

## Mission Phases

| # | Phase | Objective | Beat |
|---|-------|-----------|------|
| 1 | **Insertion** (cutscene) | — | Maru's helicopter drops Biribel in the hills above the crossing. 3D. |
| 2 | **The Customs Post** | Get through the abandoned post. | Top-down; Guard sweeps it. Find the frozen queue of cars — families who didn't make it. |
| 3 | **No-Man's-Land** | Cross the floodlit strip. | Stealth: floodlight sweeps, a timed gap. Spotted = alarm, waves from both gates. |
| 4 | **The Rail Yard** | Find Volkov before the Guard does. | Boxcar search while Guard teams close in. |
| 5 | **The Boxcar** (3D dialogue) | — | Volkov and Biribel talk as equals for the first time. The notebook. |
| 6 | **The Train** (3D, interactive) | Cover Volkov to the train. | Short in-engine sequence: suppress Guard on the platform. Volkov reaches the step — the shot. |
| 7 | **The File** | Search the Guard terminal. | Ucilev's real identity. |
| 8 | **The Call** (radio) | — | Vasile confesses; makes the offer. Biribel doesn't answer. Maru sends him to ground (→ M7, Bodi's farm). |

**Fail state:** death reloads the latest phase checkpoint. Volkov's death in
phase 6 is scripted — the player can't prevent it, but the sequence is
interactive so it feels like they tried.

## Key Scene — The Boxcar

```
VOLKOV   "You took this from me once. Read it this time."
BIRIBEL  "...Route 12. Movement order. Authorization -" (beat) "That's Vasile's code."
VOLKOV   "I tried to sell it to anyone who'd stop it. You were the one who showed up."
BIRIBEL  "You knew. In the warehouse. You knew where it would go."
VOLKOV   "I knew who you'd give it to. I didn't know how to tell you without you shooting me."
VOLKOV   "I just wanted to get out. Funny. You did too."
```

## Key Scene — The Train (3D)

```
(Platform. Snow. Volkov runs for the open carriage door. Biribel covers.)
VOLKOV   "Kim Kim has terrible coffee, I hear. I'm going to drink all of it."
(One shot from the dark. Volkov stumbles. Biribel catches him. No last words - just breath.)
(The doors close. The train leaves.)
```

## What This Resolves

- Volkov's story: from "rat" to the man who tried to stop Route 12.
- Who ordered Route 12 (Retardovici) and who carried it out (Vasile).
- Ucilev's identity.

## What It Leaves Open (For M7 / M8)

- Vasile's offer, unanswered.
- Duty or Revenge (chosen at the end of M7).

## Technical Notes

- **Package:** `src/missions/m6/`, same facade pattern; stage driver
  `STATE.mission6_stage`.
- **3D:** insertion, boxcar, train and the choice screen are 3D films on
  `engine3d`. The train sequence is interactive in 3D (aim + fire from
  Biribel's shoulder cam) — the same pattern the M8 kill scenes use, so build it
  here first as the prototype.
- **Floodlights:** cone sweeps as top-down light polygons; detection = player
  tile inside the cone for > 0.4 s.
- **New state:** `STATE.m6_alarm` (affects M8's mansion guard count —
  optional). The route choice moved to M7 (`STATE.m8_route`).
