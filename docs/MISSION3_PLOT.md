# Mission 3 — "The Vostral"

> *"Every assignment since the rumor was a bullet with my name on it. This time he's firing the gun himself."*
> — Biribel

---

## Logline

Three days after the Route 12 frame-up, Biribel wakes in an IPA black-site cell — Balaceanu's long-promised murder finally in motion. Disarmed, disavowed, and with Vasile's signal dead, he has to break himself out, turn the regime's own weapon against it, and do the one thing Balaceanu cannot survive: **prove the Vostral is true.**

---

## Where We Pick Up (Recap from M2 Ending)

M2 ended on three hard cuts:

1. **President Retardovici** on national TV: *"Route 12 will be remembered as the day KRAP discipline failed the nation."*
2. **Balaceanu**: *"KRAP operations in the highway corridor fall under government review."*
3. **IPA dispatch** — *"The KRAP operator is the primary suspect. Bring him in. Authorization IPA-Code Omega."* Door kicks in. Vasile's voice breaks apart on the radio. Blackout.

**M3 cold open: Day 4. A cell.** No gun, no squad (Gabi, Seby, Darius are dead), no Vasile on comms. Maximum vulnerability — a deliberate inversion of M1/M2, where Biribel always went in armed and backed.

---

## Setting

**The Stabilization Center** — a repurposed Y-12-era courthouse in the Acaristan interior that Balaceanu uses as a domestic black site under the legal cover of the National Stability Decree. Three layered spaces:

- **The Cell Block / Interrogation Wing** (cold open + escape) — fluorescent, claustrophobic, vertical.
- **The Archives** — the regime's real records vault: service files, seizure decrees, the Vostral itself lives here. This is M3's "notebook" — the objective artifact.
- **The Broadcast Wing** — a state TV uplink Balaceanu is about to use for a live address pinning Route 12 on Biribel *personally*, by name, on camera. The climax stage.

**Time:** A single night, ~6 in-fiction hours, racing a live broadcast.

---

## The Spine — Paying Off the Vostral

The trilogy's through-line is Balaceanu's vendetta. M1 was the artifact that *triggered* the redeployment. M2 was the *false-flag* that gave the regime its pretext. **M3 is the vendetta itself, face to face** — and its inversion.

The Vostral (the rumor Biribel leaked **six years ago**, at Y-6, as a serving soldier — it reached the press at Y-1, which is what made it a capital charge: that Balaceanu's entire decorated military career is fabricated) has never been confirmed on-screen. It's the one thing Balaceanu cannot kill his way out of, because the only copy of his service record that can prove it is **classified, and only the regime holds it.** M3's engine: get into the Archives, get the record, and get it onto the live broadcast before Balaceanu finishes his address. The hunter becomes the exposé.

## Characters & Arcs

### Biribel
Goes from disarmed prisoner to the regime's worst-case scenario: an operator with nothing left to lose and proof in his hands. For the first time he's not following Vasile's orders — he's choosing the target himself.

### Prime Minister Balaceanu
Finally on-screen as an active antagonist, not just a name on dispatches. He's been engineering this since Y-1. He's smug, careful, and *terrified of one file.* His flaw: he believes custody = victory.

### Captain Vasile (and KRAP)
KRAP is STILL an organization — disavowed, under siege, fighting for its survival and credibility. The IPA frame-up threatens to dissolve/absorb KRAP, so they've mobilized as a resistance. Vasile re-establishes contact mid-mission through a stolen IPA comms relay, bringing real assets (an off-book extraction element). The trust tension is *organizational* — KRAP soldiers following a now-disavowed commander — not personal guilt. Arc: from official chain-of-command to underground resistance.

### Sebastian Volkov
*Escaped* at the end of M1 and has been off-grid ever since. Reappears as the inside man: he's the one who got himself *arrested on purpose* to reach Biribel in the cells, because the IPA is about to disappear Biribel permanently and Volkov needs him alive to trade. An opportunist ally — his loyalty lasts exactly as long as his exit does.

### Maru (MAG)
First physical appearance. Reveal: Maru is not a guerrilla warlord; Maru is a **former IPA archivist** who faked their own death at Y-2 after refusing to forge seizure documents — the reason MAG bombs IPA checkpoints is personal, and the reason MAG was such a convenient scapegoat for Route 12 is that the regime *knew* Maru would never publicly deny it (denying = surfacing). Maru becomes the third ally — the only person besides Balaceanu who knows where the real service record is kept, because **Maru is the one who was ordered to forge it.** This is the trilogy's buried root.

### President Retardovici
Remains the public face, but M3 quietly demotes him: he's a cape Balaceanu hides behind. His TV address is the ticking clock, not the villain.
> **M5 retcon (do not "fix" this back):** the cape reading is what the characters believe on the night. M5 reveals that the cape was the puppeteer all along — Retardovici gave the Route 12 order through Vasile and used Balaceanu as his attack dog for six years. M3 and M4 are written to be re-read, not to be corrected.

## Mission Phases (6)

| # | Phase | Objective | Beat |
|---|-------|-----------|------|
| 1 | **Cold Open / Interrogation** | Survive the room. | No weapon. Dialogue-driven interrogation; Balaceanu taunts about the Vostral on a monitor. Learn the broadcast is *tonight.* Volkov engineers the cell-block breach. |
| 2 | **The Breakout** | Escape the cell block. | Stealth-first, improvised weapons (shank, taken sidearm). Re-establishes the M1 infiltration muscle but from inside-out. First IPA guards. |
| 3 | **KRAP Re-Contact** | Reach comms. | Vasile comes back over a stolen relay — broken signal, feeding floor plans. KRAP mobilized as resistance, bringing real assets. Organizational trust tension. |
| 4 | **The Archives** | Steal the Vostral file + the National Stability Decree originals. | The mission's "notebook" objective. Reveal that Maru forged the record → Maru makes contact here. Heist beat, light puzzle (reuse the M1 safe/keypad mechanics), no alarms. |
| 5 | **The Kill Order** | Survive Balaceanu's personal security sweep. | Alarms trip. IPA sends the disciplined team (M2's sniper-kill-box discipline, now hunting you in corridors). Set-piece combat. Volkov's extraction window is closing — he may bail. |
| 6 | **Live Broadcast** | Get the file onto the uplink before Balaceanu finishes his address. | Climax. Not a boss fight — a race against a countdown speech. Choose: interrupt the address on-air (max exposure, max risk), or slip the file to Vasile's off-book channel (safe, slower, may be spun). End-state branches on this choice. |

## What This Resolves

- Balaceanu's vendetta (the Vostral — proven or exposed)
- Volkov's escape from M1 (he got arrested on purpose to reach Biribel)
- Maru's identity (former IPA archivist, not a warlord)
- The National Stability Decree as root cause
- KRAP's arc from official unit to underground resistance
- Vasile's arc from commander to resistance leader

## What It Leaves Open (For a Possible M4)

- **Retardovici's fate** — he's a puppet; does the regime survive without its cape?
- **KRAP itself** — now under "government review" — does it dissolve, go fully underground, or mutiny?
- **Biribel is now a fugitive**, not a soldier — the next game's frame is no longer "mission assigned," it's "war of his own choosing."
